FeedTheBlock NYC

Feeding NYC, one block at a time.

Ever walked past someone on the train asking for a dollar and hesitated, not sure where that dollar would really go? We get it. Most of us want to help. We just want to know our money is actually doing some good.

That's why we started FeedTheBlock NYC. You give, we buy real food like bread, turkey, peanut butter, jelly, and water, and we hand it out ourselves to our unhoused neighbors, right here on our streets. No middleman, no guessing. Every food run gets posted with photos and receipts, so you see exactly where your money went.

We're New Yorkers looking out for New Yorkers. Born in Queens, built for every borough, and powered by people like you who believe a simple meal can make someone's day a little better.

Our challenge to New York: give so much that we can't keep up.
