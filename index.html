Our Purpose

Ever walked past someone on the train asking for a dollar and hesitated, not sure where that dollar would really go? We get it. Most of us want to help. We just want to know our money is actually doing some good.

That's why we started FeedTheBlock NYC. We're New Yorkers who wanted a simple, honest way to look out for our unhoused neighbors. You give, we buy the food, and we hand it out ourselves, right here on our streets. No middleman, no guessing. Every food run gets posted, so you can see exactly where your money went.

  1. You donate

    Any amount helps. Even $5 makes a difference.

  2. We buy food

    Bread, turkey, peanut butter, jelly, water, and more.

  3. We hand it out

    We go out on the streets of NYC and post proof of every food run.
