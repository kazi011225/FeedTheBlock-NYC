FeedTheBlock NYC

Feeding NYC's unhoused neighbors, one food run at a time.

Ever walked past someone on the streets asking for a dollar and hesitated, not sure where that dollar would really go?
We understand that most of us want to help. We just want to know our money is actually doing some good.

That's why we started FeedTheBlock NYC. You give, we buy real food and we hand it out ourselves to our unhoused neighbors.
No middleman, no guessing. Every food run gets posted with photos and receipts, so you see exactly where your money went.

We're New Yorkers looking out for New Yorkers, powered by people who believe a simple meal can make someone's day better.

Our challenge to New York: give so much that we can't keep up.
